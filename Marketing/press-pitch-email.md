app), The Sweet Setup, 9to5Mac (indie app coverage), iMore, Club MacStories, MacRumors.
Personalize the first line for each — one sentence referencing something they actually
published. Send individually, never CC. Attach nothing; link the press kit.

**Subject options:**
- Volume: a workout logger that gives you one number (indie, SwiftUI, Data Not Collected)
- A deliberately tiny workout app — one number, no coaching
