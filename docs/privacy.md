# Privacy Policy — Volume

**Last updated: 21 July 2026**

Volume is a workout logger. This policy explains what happens to your information. The
short version: **we don't collect any of it.**

## What we collect

Nothing.

Volume has no account system, no sign-up, no login and no server of our own. We do not
collect, receive, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information about you. We
cannot identify you.

## Where your workouts are stored

The workouts you log — your templates, exercises, sets, reps, weights and dates — are
stored **on your device**.

If you are signed into iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, Volume also stores a copy in
your **private iCloud database** so it appears on your other devices. That database belongs
to your Apple Account. It is governed by [Apple's Privacy
Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and **we have no ability to access, read or
retrieve it.** We could not hand it over to anyone if we wanted to.

If you are not signed into iCloud, Volume works exactly the same way and your workouts stay
on your device only.

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

Volume contains **no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party
SDKs**. Nothing about your usage is measured or reported. We do not track you across apps
or websites, and we do not share anything with data brokers.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store. Apple processes
the payment. **We never see your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account.**
The app only ever learns whether an active subscription exists — a yes or a no, with no
identity attached.

## Health data

Volume does not read from or write to Apple Health, and requests no health permissions.

## Deleting your data

**Settings ▸ Reset all data** inside the app permanently deletes every workout, template
and setting. Deleting the app removes its local data. To remove the synced copy, use
Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage on your device.

Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing
for us to delete on your behalf.

## Children

Volume is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone,
including children.
