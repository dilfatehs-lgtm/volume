Common questions

How is my score calculated?
Reps × weight for every set, added together. Bodyweight sets count their reps. Warm-up sets are saved but don't count toward the score.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and no login. If you're signed into iCloud your workouts sync across your own devices automatically.

What happens to my workouts if I stop subscribing?
Nothing is deleted. Your log stays exactly where it is and comes back the moment you resubscribe.

How do I cancel?
On your iPhone: Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?
In the app: Settings ▸ Reset all data. That permanently removes every workout, template and setting.
